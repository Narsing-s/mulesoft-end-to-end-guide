# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

MuleJourney is an open-source learning community. We want contributors, learners, reviewers, maintainers, and visitors to feel welcome and respected.

We are committed to providing a friendly, inclusive, and constructive environment for everyone, regardless of experience level, background, location, identity, or technical skill.

## Expected behavior

Please:

- Be respectful and professional.
- Welcome beginners and help them learn.
- Explain technical disagreements with evidence and clear reasoning.
- Give constructive feedback focused on the work, not the person.
- Credit existing work and respect licenses.
- Keep examples, documentation, issues, and pull requests relevant to the project.
- Ask questions when requirements or behavior are unclear.
- Help keep discussions useful, accessible, and safe.

## Unacceptable behavior

The following behavior is not acceptable:

- Harassment, discrimination, threats, or personal attacks.
- Trolling, deliberately disruptive behavior, or repeated bad-faith participation.
- Publishing private or sensitive information without permission.
- Spam, malicious content, or intentionally harmful contributions.
- Plagiarism or knowingly presenting someone else's work as your own.
- Any other behavior that would make the community unsafe or hostile.

## Technical disagreements

Different solutions are expected in an open-source project. When disagreeing:

1. Describe the problem clearly.
2. Explain the reasoning behind your proposal.
3. Provide reproducible examples where possible.
4. Consider alternative approaches.
5. Keep the discussion focused on improving the project.

## Reporting problems

If you experience or observe unacceptable behavior, please report it privately to the project maintainer through the repository's available contact or reporting channels. Please do not publicly post sensitive information about the people involved.

Reports should include enough context to understand what happened, along with relevant links or evidence when appropriate.

## Maintainer responsibility

Maintainers may moderate discussions, request changes, close conversations, or remove contributions that violate this Code of Conduct or materially harm the project community.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to project spaces and interactions related to MuleJourney, including repository discussions, issues, pull requests, documentation contributions, and project community channels operated by the maintainers.

Thank you for helping make MuleJourney a welcoming place to learn and contribute.
